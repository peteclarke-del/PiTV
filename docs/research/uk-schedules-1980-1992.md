UK FOUR-CHANNEL SCHEDULE SHAPES, c.1980 TO 1992: RESEARCH REPORT

0. ACCESS AND METHOD (read this first)

- BBC Genome (genome.ch.bbc.co.uk) could not be used. Every request returns an AWS WAF "Human Verification" captcha (HTTP 405, x-amzn-waf-action: captcha). I did not attempt to get past it. The Wayback Machine, World Radio History (Cloudflare challenge), TVRDb ("confirm you are human") and the Fandom Radio Times wikis (403/402) were likewise closed to automated access and I left them alone. If you want Genome data, someone needs to open those pages in a browser by hand; the URL pattern that Transdiffusion links to is genome.ch.bbc.co.uk/schedules/bbcone/london/YYYY-MM-DD and /schedules/bbctwo/england/YYYY-MM-DD.
- Real listings therefore come from Transdiffusion's "Tonight's ... in 19xx" series, which reproduces scanned TVTimes, Radio Times and newspaper listing pages. I downloaded the scans (site robots.txt allows it) and had them transcribed by reading the images. 17 channel-days of ITV/C4 and 9 channel-days of BBC were transcribed.
- Consequence: BBC coverage from real listings is limited to Wed 17 Sep 1980, Fri 25 Apr 1986, Sat 1 Nov 1986, Sun 2 Nov 1986, Sun 26 Aug 1990 and Fri 18 Oct 1991. I have NO real BBC listing for 1982-83 or 1988-89, no BBC Saturday for 1991-92, and no ITV or Channel 4 Sunday after 1980. Those cells below rest on Wikipedia timelines and strand articles and are marked [W] (general article) rather than [L] (real listing). Weekday samples are whatever day the archive held (Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri), not always a Wednesday.
